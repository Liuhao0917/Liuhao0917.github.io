About

I am a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, supervised by Prof. Zhibin Yang. My research focuses on model-driven engineering, software architecture design, and formal methods for safety-critical software systems.

I work on AADL-based architecture modeling, heterogeneous behavioral modeling, model transformation, code generation, formal analysis and verification, and the application of large language models to intelligent system modeling, architecture synthesis, and engineering automation. I am also interested in theorem proving and mechanized verification.
